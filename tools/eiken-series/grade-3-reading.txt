Making a radio program|Nao joined a school radio group because she wanted to talk about music. At first, she wrote long introductions and often ran out of time. A useful survey|The library wanted more teenagers to use its study room. Staff first thought the chairs were uncomfortable. They asked students to fill in a short survey. A new lunch choice|Students asked the school cafe to sell more healthy lunches. The cafe introduced vegetable wraps, but few students bought them during the first week. Staff discovered that the menu listed only the price, not the ingredients. They added pictures and ingredient lists. Students could see that the wraps contained familiar foods such as cheese and carrots. Sales increased without changing the recipe or the price.
